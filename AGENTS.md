# AutoTriager coursework and research

- Follow `docs/WORKSHOP_STANDARD.md` for all subsequent assignment drafts.
- Use English for submitted reports/slides; use concise Chinese for user updates.
- Preserve the instructor's required content and page limit even when using a
  workshop paper layout. Author name: Dahe Chen.
- Formulate a concrete question before experiments. Follow `research/program.md`
  for bounded autoresearch; freeze evaluation and distinguish development from
  confirmation data.
- Require an independent simulated reviewer pass before submission. Resolve
  objections against inspectable sources and results, not an AI self-score.
- Keep planned, implemented, measured, and unresolved claims separate. Citation
  validity is not causal grounding; a local demo is not production validation.
- Runtime repairs, tests, and screenshots demonstrate engineering progress;
  they do not by themselves demonstrate novelty or user benefit.
- Keep secrets, private runtime configuration, and intervention labels outside
  model input and public releases. Do not invent screenshots or experiment data.
