# Challenge 2 Delivery Status

Author: Dahe Chen. Updated October 1, 2026.

## Implemented and measured

The official Astronomy Shop runs locally, and the application loads captured
incidents, displays a saved service suggestion or deferral, exposes source
records, and offers a human judgment form. Nine public replay bundles preserve
the actual recorded answers, including the checkout counterexample.

The bounded study remains unchanged: thirty model calls produced twenty-eight
responses and two HTTP 503 failures. Nine dependent windows cover one
development and two completed confirmation groups on one fault mechanism.
Confirmation payment-label agreement was 2/2 for the direct reference and 1/2
for grounded inspection priority; both deferred on 4/4 controls. They ask
different questions, so these results do not establish same-task superiority,
optimal inspection choice, cross-fault generalization, or user benefit.

After that study, the app added **offline component comparison**. Users choose
two or three services and inspect captured records, actual direct parent-child
links, or records outside the saved model input through three predefined
questions. This does not call a model, change the earlier result, or create a
new diagnostic experiment. Its boundaries are described in
[INVESTIGATION_COMPARISON.md](INVESTIGATION_COMPARISON.md).
